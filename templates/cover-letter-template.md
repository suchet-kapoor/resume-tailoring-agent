# Cover Letter Template

Based on Uchenna's proven 5-paragraph formula.

## Template Structure

### Paragraph 1 - The Hook
Open with genuine enthusiasm for THIS specific company. Mention something specific about them.

**Template:**
"I'm excited to apply for the [ROLE TITLE] position at [COMPANY NAME]. I'm particularly drawn to [SPECIFIC COMPANY DETAIL - mission/product/challenge they're facing]."

**Example:**
"I'm excited to apply for the Business Intelligence Specialist position at TechCorp Solutions. I'm particularly drawn to your mission of democratizing data access across organizations."

### Paragraph 2 - Technical Match  
List relevant technical skills using their exact terminology. Keep it natural but keyword-rich.

**Template:**
"I bring [X years] of experience in [ROLE AREA] with strong skills in [EXACT TECHNICAL TERMS FROM JD]. My expertise includes [LIST 3-4 KEY TECHNOLOGIES/SKILLS FROM JD]."

**Example:**
"I bring 4 years of experience in business intelligence with strong SQL skills, including the ability to query, join, and manipulate large datasets. My expertise includes data visualization tools like Tableau and Power BI, Python programming, and cloud platforms."

### Paragraph 3 - Experience Story
Give a brief example of relevant work. Use numbers where possible.

**Template:**
"In my previous role at [COMPANY], I [SPECIFIC ACHIEVEMENT WITH NUMBERS]. This experience directly relates to your need for [REQUIREMENT FROM JD]."

**Example:**
"In my previous role at TechCorp, I built dashboards that reduced reporting time by 30% and provided actionable insights to over 15 departments. This experience directly relates to your need for cross-functional collaboration and data-driven decision making."

### Paragraph 4 - Why This Role
Explain what specifically excites you about this role. Reference something from the job description.

**Template:**
"What excites me most about this opportunity is [SPECIFIC ASPECT FROM JD]. I'm particularly interested in [MENTION SPECIFIC RESPONSIBILITY OR CHALLENGE]."

**Example:**
"What excites me most about this opportunity is leading data governance initiatives to ensure quality and consistency. I'm particularly interested in mentoring junior analysts and driving your bold vision for data solutions."

### Paragraph 5 - Close
Confirm practical details and express interest in discussing further.

**Template:**
"I'm available for [LOCATION/WORK ARRANGEMENT FROM JD] and excited to discuss how my background in [KEY SKILL AREA] can contribute to [COMPANY GOAL]. Thank you for your consideration."

**Example:**  
"I'm available for hybrid working in San Francisco and excited to discuss how my background in business intelligence can contribute to TechCorp's data-driven culture. Thank you for your consideration."

## Key Rules

### Tone Guidelines
- Sound like a human, not a corporate robot
- Use contractions (I'm, I've, don't) for natural flow
- Avoid "I am writing to express my interest in..." - everyone writes that
- Keep under 400 words total

### Keyword Integration
- Include exact phrases from the job description
- Use their technical terminology
- Reference their company values if mentioned
- Mirror their language style

### Personalization
- Always mention something specific about the company
- Reference specific requirements from the job posting  
- Connect your experience to their stated needs
- Address location/logistics concerns proactively

## Complete Example

**Subject: Business Intelligence Specialist Application - John Smith**

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm excited to apply for the Business Intelligence Specialist position at TechCorp Solutions. I'm particularly drawn to your mission of democratizing data access and driving data-driven decision making across organizations.

I bring 4 years of experience in business intelligence with strong SQL skills, including the ability to query, join, and manipulate large and complex datasets. My expertise includes data visualization tools like Tableau and Power BI, Python programming, and experience with cloud data platforms.

In my previous role at DataCorp, I built dashboards that reduced reporting time by 30% and delivered actionable insights to over 15 departments. This experience directly relates to your need for cross-functional collaboration and maintaining rigorous data quality standards.

What excites me most about this opportunity is leading data governance initiatives while mentoring junior analysts. I'm particularly interested in embodying your values of being Bold in driving data solutions and acting with Credible professionalism in all analysis.

I'm available for hybrid working in San Francisco (3 days per week in office) and excited to discuss how my background in business intelligence can contribute to TechCorp's data-driven culture. Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,  
John Smith
