# Cover Letter Template

Based on Uchenna's proven 5-paragraph formula.

---

## Template Structure

### Paragraph 1 - The Hook
Open with genuine enthusiasm for THIS specific company. Mention something specific about them.

**Example:**
"I am excited to apply for the [ROLE TITLE] position at [COMPANY NAME]. Your recent [SPECIFIC PROJECT/INITIATIVE/MISSION] particularly resonates with my passion for [RELEVANT AREA]."

### Paragraph 2 - Technical Match  
List relevant technical skills using their exact terminology. Keep it natural but keyword-rich.

**Example:**
"I bring strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large datasets, along with experience in Python and data visualization tools like Tableau. My [X] years of experience in [EXACT FIELD FROM JD] aligns perfectly with your requirements."

### Paragraph 3 - Experience Story
Give a brief example of relevant work. Use numbers where possible.

**Example:**  
"In my current role as [TITLE], I [SPECIFIC ACHIEVEMENT WITH NUMBERS]. This experience has prepared me to [RELATE TO JOB REQUIREMENTS] and deliver [EXPECTED OUTCOME]."

### Paragraph 4 - Why This Role
Explain what specifically excites you about this role. Reference something from the job description.

**Example:**
"I am particularly drawn to the opportunity to [SPECIFIC RESPONSIBILITY FROM JD]. The focus on [COMPANY VALUE/INITIATIVE] aligns with my commitment to [RELATED PERSONAL VALUE]."

### Paragraph 5 - Close
Confirm practical details and express interest in discussing further.

**Example:**
"I am available for [LOCATION/SCHEDULE REQUIREMENTS] and would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience in [KEY SKILL] can contribute to [COMPANY GOAL]. Thank you for your consideration."

---

## Key Rules

- Sound like a human, not a corporate robot
- Use contractions (I'm, I've, don't)  
- Avoid "I am writing to express my interest in..."
- Don't repeat your entire resume
- Keep under 400 words
- Include keywords from job description
- Show genuine company research

## Example (Filled Template)

**Subject:** Application for Business Intelligence Specialist Position

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am excited to apply for the Business Intelligence Specialist position at DataFlow Inc. Your commitment to transforming data into actionable insights that drive business decisions aligns perfectly with my passion for turning complex datasets into strategic value.

I bring strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large datasets, along with 3+ years of experience in business intelligence and data analytics. My proficiency in Python and experience with data visualization tools like Tableau directly match your technical requirements.

In my current role as Data Analyst at TechCorp, I improved reporting efficiency by 25% through automated dashboard creation and cross-functional collaboration. This experience has prepared me to drive data governance initiatives and lead analytics projects that deliver measurable business impact.

I'm particularly drawn to the opportunity to lead cross-functional analytics projects and create comprehensive reporting solutions. Your emphasis on bold innovation and collaborative teamwork resonates with my approach to data-driven problem solving.

I am available for remote work and would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience in business intelligence can contribute to DataFlow's continued growth. Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]

**Word count:** 187 words
