# Resume Format Guidelines

ATS-friendly formatting requirements based on Uchenna's research.

## File Requirements

### Format
- **Use:** .docx files only
- **Avoid:** PDF (unless specifically requested)
- **Why:** Most ATS systems parse .docx better

### Fonts
- **Use:** Calibri, Arial, or Times New Roman
- **Size:** 10-12pt for body text
- **Headers:** Can be slightly larger (14pt max)
- **Avoid:** Fancy fonts, decorative fonts

### Layout
- **Single column only** - No two-column layouts
- **Standard margins** - 1 inch (2.54cm) all sides
- **Left-aligned text** - No center or right alignment for body text
- **Clear hierarchy** - Use consistent heading styles

## Structure Requirements

### Section Headers
Use standard headers that ATS systems recognize:
- PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
- EDUCATION  
- SKILLS
- CERTIFICATIONS (if applicable)

### Bullet Points
- **Use:** Standard bullets (•)
- **Avoid:** Custom symbols, arrows, checkmarks
- **Format:** Consistent spacing and alignment

### Contact Information
- Place at top of document
- Include: Name, phone, email, location
- **Avoid:** Headers/footers (ATS often can't read them)

## What to Avoid

### Graphics & Design
- ❌ No images, photos, logos
- ❌ No tables or columns  
- ❌ No text boxes
- ❌ No borders or lines
- ❌ No background colors
- ❌ No infographics or charts

### Special Characters
- ❌ No unusual symbols or icons
- ❌ No special formatting characters
- ❌ No embedded objects

### Templates
- ❌ Avoid Canva templates
- ❌ Avoid fancy Word templates
- ❌ Avoid creative resume builders

## Best Practices

### Text Formatting
- **Bold:** Use sparingly for job titles and section headers
- **Italics:** Use for company names and dates
- **ALL CAPS:** Use only for section headers
- **Underlining:** Avoid entirely

### Spacing
- Single line spacing within sections
- Double line spacing between sections
- Consistent indentation for bullet points

### File Naming
- Use clear, professional names
- Example: "John_Smith_Business_Intelligence_Specialist.docx"
- Include role title for easy identification

## Quick Checklist

Before submitting any resume:

✅ File saved as .docx  
✅ Standard font (Calibri/Arial/Times New Roman)  
✅ Single column layout  
✅ No graphics or tables  
✅ Standard section headers  
✅ Standard bullet points (•)  
✅ Contact info at top (not in header)  
✅ Consistent formatting throughout  
✅ Professional file name  

## Why This Matters

- **ATS parsing:** Simple formats parse correctly
- **Keyword recognition:** Standard layouts improve scanning
- **Human review:** Clean format is easier to read
- **Compatibility:** Works across all ATS systems

Remember: Your resume content is brilliant - don't let formatting prevent it from being seen.
