# Tailoring Strategies

## The Complete Uchenna Methodology

Based on "How I Got 6 Interviews in One Week" - proven strategies that work.

## Core Principles

### 1. Your CV is NOT About You
**It's about proving you match what they're looking for.**

Everything stems from this principle.

### 2. One CV Per Application (Non-Negotiable)
- Never send the same CV twice
- Every application gets a freshly tailored CV
- Increase match score from 30% to 85%+

### 3. The Verbatim Keyword Strategy
Copy EXACT phrases from job descriptions. Not paraphrased. Not synonyms. Exact words.

**Example:**
- Job Description: "Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large and complex datasets"
- Your CV: "Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large and complex datasets"

## Advanced Strategies

### 4. Mirror Job Description Structure
If their JD has sections like:
- "Data Strategy & Leadership"  
- "Data Governance & Quality"
- "Analytics & Insight Generation"

Then your experience section uses those EXACT headers with bullets underneath.

### 5. The Role Title Trick
Change your job title to match the role you're applying for (if reasonably accurate).

**Examples:**
- Applying for "Data Governance Specialist"? → CV title becomes "Data Governance Specialist"
- Applying for "Analytics Lead, Operations"? → CV title becomes "Analytics Lead, Operations"

**Important:** Only do this if your responsibilities genuinely match.

### 6. Values Alignment Trick
Many companies list values in job descriptions. Create a dedicated CV section:

**Values Alignment**
• Bold: Act with ambition, courage and determination in driving data transformation
• Credible: Act with rigour and professionalism in implementing quality standards  
• Human: Act to have positive impact through training and stakeholder support
• Together: Act inclusively with data owners, stewards, and cross-functional teams

### 7. Quantify Everything
Numbers stand out on CVs:
- "Achieved 3,050% ROI on investments"
- "25% increase in forecast accuracy" 
- "30% reduction in reporting discrepancies"
- "Datasets exceeding 10 million records"
- "Across 15 departments"
- "Within 12 months"

### 8. Location and Logistics
Address concerns proactively in "Additional Information":
- For hybrid: "Available for hybrid working in London (3 days per week in office)"
- For relocation: "Open to relocation to Berlin, Germany"
- For visa: "UK resident with full right to work in the UK"

## Cover Letter Formula (5 Paragraphs)

### Paragraph 1 - The Hook
Open with genuine enthusiasm for THIS specific company. Mention something specific about them.

### Paragraph 2 - Technical Match  
List relevant technical skills using their exact terminology. Keep it natural but keyword-rich.

### Paragraph 3 - Experience Story
Brief example of relevant work with numbers. "Built dashboards that reduced reporting time by 30%"

### Paragraph 4 - Why This Role
What specifically excites you about THIS role. Reference something from the job description.

### Paragraph 5 - Close
Confirm practical details and express interest in discussing further. Keep it short.

## ATS System Specifics

### Greenhouse
- Uses keyword matching + scorecard alignment
- Pays attention to role title matches
- Has structured knockout questions

### Workable  
- Keyword scoring + skills matching
- Has disqualification questions
- Parses .docx well

### Lever
- Focused on skills and experience duration
- Make sure years of experience are clearly stated
- Cover letters matter here

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't use generic "one size fits all" CV  
❌ Don't use fancy templates with graphics/columns  
❌ Don't skip cover letter when optional  
❌ Don't use vague statements without numbers  
❌ Don't apply to roles you're not genuinely interested in  
❌ Don't wait days to apply after seeing a job  

## Success Checklist

Before submitting ANY application:

✅ Does CV title match job title?  
✅ Have I included verbatim keywords from JD?  
✅ Does experience section mirror their requirements structure?  
✅ Have I included quantifiable achievements?  
✅ Is CV in ATS-friendly format (.docx, single column)?  
✅ Have I addressed location/logistics concerns?  
✅ Does cover letter show genuine interest in THIS company?  
✅ Have I used their exact technical terminology?  
✅ Have I aligned with their stated values?  
✅ Am I applying quickly after job was posted?

## The Speed Factor

Apply to jobs as quickly as possible after they're posted:
- Many companies review on rolling basis
- Early applicants get more attention  
- Some roles close early when they find good candidates
- Recruiter fatigue sets in after hundreds of applications

## Results

This approach secured **6 interviews in one week** because it stops competing with hundreds of generic applicants and starts standing out as someone who clearly understands what the company needs.
