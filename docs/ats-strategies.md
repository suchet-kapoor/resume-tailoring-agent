# ATS Strategies

Based on Uchenna's proven methodology.

## Key Rules

### 1. Verbatim Keywords
Copy EXACT phrases from job descriptions. Not paraphrased. Not synonyms. Exact words.

**Example:**
- Job says: "Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large datasets"  
- Resume says: "Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large datasets"

### 2. Role Title Matching
Change your job title to match the target role (if accurate).

**Example:**
- Applying for "Data Analyst"? → Your title becomes "Data Analyst"
- Applying for "Business Intelligence Specialist"? → Your title becomes "Business Intelligence Specialist"

### 3. Mirror Job Structure
Structure your experience sections to match their job categories.

If they list:
- Data Strategy & Leadership
- Analytics & Reporting  
- Process Improvement

Then your experience uses those exact headers.

### 4. ATS-Friendly Format
- File: .docx (not PDF)
- Font: Calibri, Arial, or Times New Roman
- Layout: Single column only
- No tables, graphics, or fancy formatting
- Standard bullet points (•)

### 5. One Resume Per Job
Never send the same resume twice. Every job gets a tailored version.

## Why This Works
- ATS systems score on keyword matching
- Generic resumes miss 60-80% of company-specific terms
- Tailored resumes can score 85%+ match rates
