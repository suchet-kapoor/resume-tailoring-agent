# Sample Job Description

This is an example job posting that demonstrates key elements for tailoring.

---

## Business Intelligence Specialist - Data Team
**TechCorp Solutions | Remote | Full-time**

### About the Role
We're seeking a Business Intelligence Specialist to join our growing data team. You'll work cross-functionally to drive data-driven decision making across the organization.

### What You'll Do
**Business Intelligence & Analytics**
- Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large and complex datasets
- Experience with data visualization tools like Tableau, Power BI, or similar platforms
- Build and maintain dashboards that deliver actionable insights to stakeholders

**Data Strategy & Leadership** 
- Collaborate cross-functionally with product, marketing, and operations teams
- Lead data governance initiatives to ensure data quality and consistency
- Mentor junior analysts and support their professional development

**Technical Requirements**
- 3+ years of experience in business intelligence or data analysis
- Proficiency in SQL, Python, and Excel
- Experience with cloud data platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Knowledge of statistical analysis and A/B testing methodologies

### Our Values
We're looking for someone who embodies our core values:
- **Bold**: Act with ambition and determination in driving data solutions
- **Credible**: Maintain rigorous standards and professionalism in all analysis
- **Human**: Focus on positive impact for people through data insights
- **Together**: Collaborate inclusively across teams and departments

### Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field
- Available for hybrid working in San Francisco (3 days per week in office)
- Strong communication skills and ability to present to senior leadership
- Experience with agile development methodologies

### Nice to Have
- Master's degree in quantitative field
- Experience with machine learning tools
- Previous consulting or client-facing experience

---

- Change resume title to "Business Intelligence Specialist"
