## Business Intelligence Specialist
**Company:** DataFlow Inc.  
**Location:** Remote  

### About the Role
We are seeking a Business Intelligence Specialist to join our analytics team. You will be responsible for transforming data into actionable insights that drive business decisions.

### What you'll do
- **Data Strategy & Leadership:** Drive data governance initiatives and lead cross-functional analytics projects
- **Analytics & Reporting:** Create comprehensive dashboards and automated reporting solutions  
- **Business Intelligence:** Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large and complex datasets
- **Data Visualization:** Experience with data visualization tools like Tableau, Power BI, or similar platforms

### Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in business intelligence or data analytics
- Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large datasets
- Experience with Python, R, or similar analytical programming languages
- Knowledge of data warehouse concepts and ETL processes
- Excellent communication skills and ability to present findings to stakeholders

### Company Values
- **Bold:** Act with courage and determination in driving innovation
- **Credible:** Maintain rigorous standards and professional integrity  
- **Collaborative:** Work together to achieve shared goals
