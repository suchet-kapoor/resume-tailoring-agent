## After Tailoring (Example)

When tailored for a "Business Intelligence Specialist" role, this becomes:

## John Smith
**Business Intelligence Specialist**  
**Email:** john.smith@email.com  
**Phone:** (555) 123-4567  

### Business Intelligence & Analytics
- Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, join, and manipulate large datasets
- Experience with data visualization tools including Excel and Python
- Delivered actionable insights that improved reporting efficiency by 25%

### Data Strategy & Leadership  
- Collaborated cross-functionally to drive data-driven decision making
- Supported strategic initiatives through comprehensive data analysis
- Mentored junior team members on analytical best practices

### Technical Proficiency
- SQL, Excel, Python for data manipulation and analysis
- Database management and data extraction
- Statistical analysis and business intelligence reporting

**Notice:** The tailored version uses exact phrases from the job description and restructures content to match the target role's requirements.
